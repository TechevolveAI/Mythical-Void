WHAT PLAYERS DO

Adventure, care and restoration.

Players run, jump, explore, solve problems and take part in fantasy battles. The target is the purple corruption holding each guardian—not the guardian itself.

01

Hatch a creature

Procedural genetics shape its look, affinity, personality and potential. The game does not claim that a creature is conscious or alive.

02

Restore six realms

Players explore six worlds, clear corruption, help their guardians and rebuild parts of the shared village.

03

Make story choices

The journey changes how the player thinks about Project Beacon, Earth and the creature that trusted them.

04

Discover real space

Optional moments can show credited NASA public images or space information and invite observation and questions.

Curiosity without pretending fiction is fact.

Optional discovery moments name and credit their NASA source, then place the creature’s fictional response in a separate “Mythical Void imagines” area. Players are asked to observe, infer and think about how they could check an idea.

Important: NASA does not endorse Mythical Void. Live public data may not always be available, and the fictional world remains fictional.

QUICK QUESTIONS

Clear answers before play.

Does a child need to create an account?

No. The first creature can be hatched without sharing a name or email address. Cloud Save is optional and should only be used with any adult permission required where the player lives.

What is stored?

Progress is stored in the current browser by default. If Cloud Save is chosen, an anonymous account number, game save and save times are stored. See the privacy page for the current details.

What does the combat contain?

The current adventure contains family-friendly fantasy battles without gore. That is a description of the game, not a formal age rating or a promise that every child will react in the same way.

Where is AI used?

AI helps the small studio explore ideas and build some optional creative moments. The main story and important choices are written and checked by people. Age-gated experimental media is not needed to play the main adventure.

Can I contact the studio?

The official parent and guardian contact channel is still being prepared. Until it opens, this page and the privacy page are the current public sources of information.
