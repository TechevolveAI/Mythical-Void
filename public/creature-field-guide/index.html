FIELD-GUIDE STORY

The Memory Gardener

Crystal Elemental

  • Common
  • Wise nature
  • Moon affinity
  • Blob form
  • Void Patches

Old forest signals gather in the dark patches across this small Crystal Elemental. It does not hoard them. Under moonlight, it carries each fading rhythm to a root that has forgotten how to answer, then waits—quiet and wise—until the Current begins again.

Beacon note: A living archive may need a gardener, not an owner.
MV-0715 // REAL GAME RENDER

FIELD-GUIDE STORY

The Wyrm Who Reads the Roots

Stellar Wyrm

  • Legendary
  • Curious nature
  • Nebula affinity
  • Serpentine form
  • Extra Eyes
  • Inanimate Markings

Circuit-like marks travel along this Stellar Wyrm as if the forest is writing through it. Its floating eyes follow different branches of the same signal. When Wanderer-77 first disturbs the roots, the curious Wyrm does not attack—it traces the new noise back to the crash.

Beacon note: First contact begins when both sides notice the disturbance.

FIELD-GUIDE STORY

The Prism That Would Not Be Taken

Cosmic Guardian

  • Legendary
  • Gentle nature
  • Crystal affinity
  • Quadruped form
  • Gem Body
  • Elemental Aura
  • Prismatic Sheen

A ruby-bright body and prismatic sheen made this Cosmic Guardian look like the richest object in the cavern. Every extraction tool agreed. The gentle creature answered by standing between the drill and a wounded crystal seam, refusing to move until the scanner learned to recognise the cave as alive.

Beacon note: Value is not permission.
MV-0567 // REAL GAME RENDER

FIELD-GUIDE STORY

The Quiet Facet

Crystal Drake

  • Uncommon
  • Wise nature
  • Crystal affinity
  • Blob form
  • Soft Glow

This wise Crystal Drake has no sharp silhouette and no interest in treasure. Its soft form settles beside unstable shards and hums at the cave’s hidden frequency. Miners once followed the loudest resonance. The Quiet Facet teaches them to listen for the note that is missing.

Beacon note: A damaged system is sometimes revealed by silence.

Crystal Drake

  • Common

  • Crystal affinity
  • Biped form
  • Floating Parts

Floating rings turn around this playful Crystal Drake whenever two distant reef signals find each other. It races the tidal Current between settlements, carrying no message of its own. Its gift is keeping the path open long enough for every smaller voice to pass.

Beacon note: Communication is infrastructure, not just information.

The Trail Beneath Water

Crystal Elemental

  • Epic
  • Gentle nature
  • Crystal affinity
  • Serpentine form
  • Feather Mane
  • Nebula Trails

The aquatic head of this serpentine Crystal Elemental senses pressure changes before the reef can show them. A flowing nebula trail marks the safest passage, then slowly disappears so predators cannot follow. The route is guidance, never a permanent claim on the water.

The Warm Stone That Moved

Nebula Sprite

  • Epic
  • Energetic nature
  • Void affinity
  • Blob form

A rough little shape sleeps among cold rocks until a warning crosses the Current. Then an ember aura wakes beneath its stone texture and it rolls toward the threatened settlement. Nobody remembers appointing the Nebula Sprite. The network simply knows who will answer.

The Last Light Under Ice

Aurora Phoenix

  • Rare
  • Playful nature
  • Star affinity
  • Winged form
  • Aurora Wing Tips

This winged Aurora Phoenix carries a star affinity inside a shadow-soft aura. Nebula Sprite

  • Rare
  • Curious nature
  • Void affinity
  • Blob form

Scale armour protects a curious Nebula Sprite whose strange appendages resemble little flames. It can cross the boundary and leave, yet always returns to the place the instruments call empty. The creature knows what Project Beacon is still learning: an unmapped home is not an unoccupied one.

Beacon note: Unknown does not mean unclaimed by life.

Branching cosmic horns hold the reflections of every restored realm. This playful, moon-bound Crystal Drake appears where the final signal divides: Earth in one direction, the Fend in another. It offers no answer. It keeps both lights visible while the astronaut chooses.
