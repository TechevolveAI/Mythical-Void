Look for the ingredients, not a score.

The challenge is not about getting a “better” creature. It is about noticing how the system can combine parts into different living possibilities.

QUICK ANSWERS

Keep the invitation simple.

Is this multiplayer?

No. Mythical Void is a single-player game. Each person plays in their own browser and compares the result afterwards.

Are two different creatures guaranteed?

No. The engine combines many varied ingredients, but Mythical Void does not promise that every possible hatch is globally unique or that two results can never match.

Does Mythical Void know who I invited?

No. The invitation uses your device’s normal sharing choices or copies a clean public link. Mythical Void does not collect the recipient.

Is anything uploaded?

No creature, save, name or picture is uploaded by this challenge. Progress stays in each player’s own browser by default.
