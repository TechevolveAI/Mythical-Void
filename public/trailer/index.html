WHAT AWAITS

Hatch. Explore. Restore. Choose.

Mythical Void is a free science-fantasy creature adventure that runs in your browser.

Recover the Wanderer-77, hatch an unusual alien creature, cross six damaged realms and fight the purple corruption trapping each guardian. What you repair—and what you tell Earth—helps decide what Project Beacon becomes.

Optional discoveries can bring real NASA space images and information into the journey as child-friendly STEM questions. NASA does not endorse Mythical Void.

FROM A FAMILY IDEA TO A REAL GAME

A dad. His nine-year-old son. One enormous question.

Mythical Void began at home in Ireland: what could generative AI tools and two imaginations build together?

That experiment grew into creatures, code, worlds, music and a game. AI helps a very small team explore and build; people remain responsible for the story, safety and every important decision.
