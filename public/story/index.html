THE EARTH DIRECTIVE

“Earth comes first.”

Find viable energy, minerals and biological samples. Restore the uplink. Report every sign of life.

That is the order which survives the crash. Earth is desperate, and the astronaut has been sent to bring back a reason for hope.

But the first sign of life is not a sample. It is an egg. The hatchling watches, responds and avoids the scanner. Your first job is not to catalogue it—it is to approach gently, offer care and earn trust.

The question underneath the game: what happens when the world you were sent to use becomes a home you are responsible for protecting?

FIRST CONTACT

A living signal answers.

The creature is intelligent, vulnerable and the first alien life to trust a human.

Your scanner cannot explain everything it sees. Care comes before collection. The creature then guides you toward pulses moving through the planet’s living Current—and eventually to a gate beyond the crash site.

THE JOURNEY

A mission that changes as you do.

  1. 01
    Survive the crash

    Recover the field kit, restore your footing and find out what survived aboard Wanderer-77.

  2. 02
    Earn trust

    Care for the hatchling and learn to follow the living signals it understands before you do.

  3. 03
    Cross six realms

    Run, leap, explore and face the purple corruption trapping each world’s guardian.

  4. 04
    Restore, don’t conquer

    Clear the pressure around a guardian. The ship parts you receive are gifts after rescue—not samples taken by force.

  5. 05
    Choose what home means

    Rebuild the uplink, understand who else is listening and decide what Project Beacon should become.

THE GUARDIANS

The fight is against the corruption.

The guardian is not the health bar you are trying to empty.

Each guardian has been wounded or trapped by purple Void pressure. Learn its attacks, stand your ground with your creature and reduce the corruption around it. When that pressure reaches zero, the guardian returns—and the world can move forward.

Rescued guardians can later become residents and take part in the life you rebuild in the Fend. Restoration changes more than a level-complete screen.

REPAIR CHANGES THE STORY

The ship is not the only thing you rebuild.

Recovered systems repair Wanderer-77 while rescued beings and useful structures create a shared home in the Fend.

What you repair first helps shape Project Beacon. By the time the signal can reach Earth, you know the coordinates could bring help—but they could also expose a living world to everyone listening.

What is the final choice about?

It is about responsibility: what Earth deserves to know, what this world deserves to keep, and whether “home” still means the place the astronaut left behind. The exact ending remains yours to reach.
