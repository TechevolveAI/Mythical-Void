OFFICIAL PRESS & CREATOR ROOM

The quick way to understand Mythical Void.

Mythical Void is a free creature adventure that runs in a browser. It began as a father-and-son idea in Ireland and grew into six strange worlds, a creature genetics system and a choice about what Earth should learn.

THE SHORT VERSION

What do you actually do?

Crash beyond mapped space. Hatch an alien creature. Cross six damaged worlds and free the Guardians trapped by the Void.

01

Hatch a creature

Body, colour, nature, cosmic affinity and rare changes combine through the game’s creature genetics.

02

Explore together

Move, jump, fight corruption, find routes and bring each strange world back to life.

03

Restore Guardians

The Guardians are trapped by corruption. The goal is to free them, not destroy them.

04

Make the final choice

Your journey helps decide what Project Beacon should tell Earth about the life found beyond it.

TRY IT BEFORE YOU WRITE

A useful first look.

Open the real game, follow the crash-site story, hatch and name a creature, then move together in the Sanctuary.

That opening is the clearest way to judge whether the idea works. A keyboard is recommended for later platforming sections. On a phone, the game provides touch controls.

Two honest questions: Was the first minute clear without an explanation? Did movement and the controls make sense on the device you used?

WHY IT EXISTS

A father-and-son experiment became a game.

Kevin and his son began by asking what imagination and today’s generative AI tools might help them build together.

Their conversations became alien creatures, impossible worlds and playable systems. AI helps the small studio explore and build. People remain responsible for the story, safety, public claims and release decisions.

DESCRIBE IT ACCURATELY

Ready-to-use wording.

Mythical Void is a free early-access browser adventure from an independent Irish studio. Players hatch a genetically varied alien creature, explore six living worlds, fight the corruption holding their Guardians and decide what Project Beacon should tell Earth.

Useful terms: creature adventure, procedural creature genetics, browser game and AI-assisted creative work.

Please avoid claims that creatures are sentient, every hatch is globally unique, generated artwork is gameplay, or NASA is a partner.
