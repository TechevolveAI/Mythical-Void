THE SHORT DESCRIPTION
Mythical Void is a free early-access browser adventure from an independent Irish studio. Players hatch a genetically varied alien creature, explore six living realms, fight the corruption holding their guardians and decide what Project Beacon should become.

FORMAT
- Browser game
- Single player
- Free current early-access release
- No download or account required to start
- Desktop or mobile browser; keyboard recommended for platforming

THE GAME
- Recover the Wanderer-77 expedition after its crash.
- Hatch and care for a creature shaped by procedural genetics.
- Explore and restore six living realms.
- Fight the Void corruption rather than destroy the guardians it holds.
- Make choices about what Project Beacon should become.
- Discover optional learning moments using selected NASA public space data.

THE STUDIO STORY
Mythical Void began as a father-and-son project in Ireland. Kevin and his nine-year-old son explored what generative AI tools and a lot of imagination could make possible. Their conversations became strange creatures, living worlds and playable moments. AI helps the small team explore and build; people remain responsible for the story, safety and important choices.
