THE MYTHICAL VOID STORY

A dad. His nine-year-old son. One enormous idea.

Mythical Void began at home with Kevin and his son asking what generative AI tools and a lot of imagination could make possible. Their conversations became strange creatures, impossible worlds and, eventually, a game anyone can play.

WHERE IT STARTED

Not with a business plan. With a question.

“What if we could build the game we imagined together?”

Ideas arrived through ordinary conversations: what a creature might look like, what power it could have, which impossible world it might protect and what choices would really matter to a child.

Generative AI tools helped turn some of those ideas into pictures, code, movement and playable scenes. Each new result created another question. The experiment grew into Wanderer-77, the Fend, Project Beacon and six damaged realms waiting to be restored.

The project is now becoming an independent game studio, but the original test still matters: can new tools help a very small team build something imaginative, ambitious and worth caring about?

FROM IDEA TO PLAYABLE WORLD

The work has to become a real game.

Mythical Void is not a gallery of generated pictures. The current release includes movement, platforming, combat, creature genetics, six realms, guardians, village building and story choices inside a Phaser browser game.

Proof matters: public gameplay images and video are captured from named game builds. Generated marketing artwork is labelled so nobody has to guess what they are seeing.

HOW WE WANT TO BUILD

Small team. Big imagination. Clear responsibility.

01

Imagination leads

Tools should help explore ideas, not flatten every story into the same familiar answer.

02

AI helps

AI can speed up research, drafting, art exploration, coding and testing. It does not remove human responsibility.

03

Evidence earns claims

If something is called gameplay, it comes from the game. If an image is generated artwork, the label says so.

04

Children deserve care

Safety, privacy and adult oversight are built into decisions because younger players should never carry the studio’s risks.

THE NEXT CHAPTER

An independent studio that can keep learning.

The aim is not to look enormous. It is to make a small studio capable: useful automation for repeated work, clear approval points for important decisions, honest public evidence and direct learning from players and families.

Social channels and contact addresses are still being prepared. Until they open, the live game and the official press room are the best ways to understand what Mythical Void is today.
