ALIEN, NOT JUST ANIMAL

Shapes from impossible realms.

The creature universe can imagine organisms shaped by their environment while keeping a playable silhouette underneath.

Could a creature feel liquid or radial?

Yes. A supported game shape can be imagined as a flowing membrane, a radial light-being, a gravity-folded organism or something made from crystal, weather or memory.

Does every idea become a game sprite?

No. Marketing artwork explores the wider universe. The actual browser creature must still fit the movement, animation and collision shapes supported by the current game.

Are generated portraits required?

No. The pixel creature is the main identity and the game can be played without optional generated media.

Is the creature an AI person?

No. The game may create responsive and memorable moments, but Mythical Void does not describe its creatures as sentient or conscious.
