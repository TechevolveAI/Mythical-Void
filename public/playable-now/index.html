WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

A child’s imagination became a living game world.

Mythical Void began in Ireland as a father-and-son experiment: Kevin and his nine-year-old son exploring what imagination and generative AI tools could make possible.

The creatures are shaped through structured genetics—body forms, colours, personalities, cosmic affinities and mutations—not chosen from a short list of mascots. Optional NASA public data can also open little doors from the fictional universe into real space discovery.

AI helps this tiny studio explore and build. People remain responsible for the story, the safety boundaries, the public claims and the choices that matter.

QUICK ANSWERS

Before you enter.

Is Mythical Void free?

Yes. The current early-access browser game is free to play.

Do I need an account or download?

No. Start in a modern browser without an account, email address or download. Progress is saved in that browser by default.

Why is there no gameplay video here right now?

The earlier clip was genuine but did not show a strong enough moment of play. It has been withdrawn while a clearer, fully reviewed replacement is made.

Is the game finished?

It is playable now and still in early access. The core adventure, six realms, guardians, building and Project Beacon journey work today; the studio continues improving them.

Is it suitable for younger players?

It is designed as a family-friendly adventure with fantasy battles but no gore. Mythical Void does not currently claim a formal age rating. Grown-ups can read the full family notes before play.

Does NASA make or endorse the game?

No. Mythical Void can use selected public NASA material for optional learning moments, with sources and credits shown. NASA does not endorse Mythical Void.
