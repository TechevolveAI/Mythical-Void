THE LEARNING PROMISE

Make the boundary part of the fun.

A child should never have to guess which part came from a scientific source and which part came from the story.

01

Name the source

Real images and information show their source, date and available credit inside the discovery card.

02

Separate imagination

The creature’s fictional reaction lives in its own clearly labelled “Mythical Void imagines” area.

03

Invite a question

Prompts focus on noticing, wondering and testing an idea rather than memorising an answer.

04

Keep it optional

Space discovery adds another layer for curious players; it is not required to understand the main adventure.

WHAT MAY APPEAR

A changing window onto space.

Astronomy pictures

Selected public images can connect real planets, stars, galaxies and missions to something the player has just seen or wondered about.

Mars images

Public Mars rover images can help players compare an imagined realm with a real landscape beyond Earth when a suitable source is available.

Space-weather information

Public solar and space-weather signals can become a prompt about the changing environment around Earth. Live availability is never guaranteed.

What this does not mean

Mythical Void is not an official NASA product, does not claim NASA endorsement and does not present its creatures or worlds as scientific fact.
