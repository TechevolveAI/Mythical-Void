## Required fields for a new experiment

- owner and decision served;
- falsifiable hypothesis;
- defined audience and recruitment source;
- intervention and comparison;
- primary measure and minimum useful sample;
- safety, privacy, brand, cost, and stop guardrails;
- data collection/retention plan;
- start/end date;
- result with uncertainty;
- decision, follow-up, and archive link.
