# Customer Evidence Register

No customer evidence has been entered yet.

Repository observations, market research, simulated personas, agent opinions,
and Kevin's product intent belong in their own sources. They do not count as
customer evidence.

The machine-readable source for the A-005 pilot is
[`../customer/evidence.json`](../customer/evidence.json). It starts empty on
purpose. A-005 validates acceptance, human review, provenance, controlled
classification, de-identification assertions, retention, and obvious leakage;
then aggregates only structured themes. It never turns repository observations
or an empty register into customer insight.
