# Mythical studio technology story

This is the plain-language source of truth for explaining why Mythical Void is unusual. It is for the website, partner conversations, press notes, and future content drafts.

## The three layers

### 1. Creature genetics

`src/systems/CreatureGenetics.js` builds a creature profile from species, body shape, colours, wings, eyes, personality tendencies, rarity, and cosmic affinity. The result is a structured identity that the game can use across care, bonding, fusion, portraits, and story systems. It is not a promise that every possible creature is infinitely unique.

### 2. The playable world

Phaser 3 runs the browser game: movement, exploration, combat, guardians, village systems, menus, effects, and story scenes. The game remains playable without an AI service. A network or generation failure must not erase the player’s progress or stop the authored adventure.

### 3. AI-made expression

The living portrait and companion-media services can create optional portraits or short AI-generated cinematic cutscenes from the player’s in-game identity. These systems are experimental, age-gated, bounded by consent and service rules, and separate from the core story. AI adds expression; it does not decide the player’s ending, impersonate a child, or replace human-authored dialogue and safety decisions.

## What we may say

- “Your companion is assembled from a living genetics system.”
- “Phaser 3 brings the world to life in your browser.”
- “Optional AI media can turn your companion’s identity into a portrait or short cinematic moment.”
- “The story and its important choices remain authored and understandable.”

## What we must not say without new evidence

- “Every creature is completely unique.”
- “The AI writes the whole game.”
- “The companion is conscious or unrestricted.”
- “AI-generated cutscenes replace the game’s authored story.”
- “A generated image proves gameplay.”

Every public claim still needs a current proof record and Kevin’s approval before publication.
