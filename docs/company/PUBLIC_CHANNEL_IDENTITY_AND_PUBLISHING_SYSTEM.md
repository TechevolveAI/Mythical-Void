evidence that a Twitter/X account exists or belongs to Mythical.

## Verification standard

An account becomes official in the company system only when a named human
records:

- its exact canonical URL;
- authenticated owner/admin access and recovery control;
- an official-domain reciprocal link or equivalent independent identity proof;
- handle, display name, description, recovery, and collision review;
- scoped roles, credential removal owner, and multi-factor authentication;
- no unresolved impersonation or naming ambiguity.

A screenshot, search-result match, matching display name, editable repository
file, conversation, or generated approval string is insufficient.

## Account creation

Before creating an account, Mythical must decide D-011, review name/handle and
trademark collisions, name an owner and backup, establish recovery/MFA, review
platform terms and age/privacy/generated-media/advertising rules, establish
moderation and incident coverage, separate publishing and analytics roles, and
prepare one complete evidence-led content package.

The recommended sequence is:

1. verify any accounts Kevin already controls;
2. keep the owned website as canonical truth;
3. after authentic gameplay proof, evaluate one video property;
4. after the company/runtime story is accurate, evaluate one professional
   company page;
5. defer live community and email until their safeguarding and consent systems
   are operational.

No handle reservation or account creation is authorized by this sequence.

## Publishing preflight

CQ-006 on CH-001 is the first bounded text-package candidate, but it is not
publication-ready. A-034 now provides its complete internal CMP-001 preview
across CH-001 and CH-004; this satisfies source, fiction-boundary, destination,
and current-text accessibility checks only. Every publication requires verified identity, owner/backup,
recovery/MFA, credential scope, moderation/safeguarding, privacy-safe
measurement, accepted claims/proof, accessibility, generated-media disclosure,
complete preview, trusted approval, tested publisher/kill switch, active reply
and incident coverage, and post-publication reconciliation.

Changing copy, media, account, link, audience, timing, disclosure, paid status,
or destination invalidates approval. Conversation is not authorization.
