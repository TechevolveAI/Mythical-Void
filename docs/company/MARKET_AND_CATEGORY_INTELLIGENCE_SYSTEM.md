## Purpose

Mythical needs a continuous view of the category without confusing a web
search, competitor claim, generated persona, or agent opinion with customer
demand. A-028 maintains a sourced reference set, separates observed product
facts from Mythical's inferences, identifies falsifiable whitespace
hypotheses, and routes them into research, proof, search, distribution, and
commercial decisions.

The workflow is read-only. It cannot contact a company, join a community,
create an account, scrape restricted data, copy protected assets, publish a
comparison, change positioning, set pricing, target an audience, or represent
its sample as market share.

## Category frame

Mythical sits across five established or emerging lanes:

1. **Browser virtual pets and persistent worlds** — immediate access,
   collection, customization, care, and long-running community.
2. **Systemic and procedural pet simulation** — surprising creature variation,
   breeding, expressive behavior, and player-created stories.
3. **Creature exploration and restoration adventures** — discovery, empathy,
   environmental stakes, action, and a complete authored journey.
4. **Large-scale creature-catching RPGs** — broad worlds, combat, collection,
   progression, and platform-scale production.
5. **AI characters and companions** — conversation, memory, autonomous
   behavior, animation, and rapidly rising expectation and trust risk.

These lanes are not a total addressable market and the reference products are
not declared direct competitors. They are comparison points for player promise,
interaction, access, proof, business model, and trust.

## Directional observations

The current first-party sample shows:

- browser-native pet adoption, care, customization, collecting, and world
  exploration are familiar propositions;
- procedural variation and breeding can make creature outcomes legible and
  shareable without promising mathematical uniqueness;
- creature discovery, empathy, flight, restoration, and action-adventure can
  carry a broader journey beyond pet care;
- major upcoming products compete on open-world scale, collection, combat, and
  a distinctive transformation mechanic;
- creator-native mechanics such as Twitch integration can be designed into the
  product rather than added as promotional plumbing;
- AI-character infrastructure is increasingly presented through planning,
  action, conversation, guidance, and animation, which raises both audience
  expectations and the burden of precise claims.

No conclusion about demand, retention, revenue, market size, audience overlap,
or relative quality follows from those observations.

## Mythical's hypotheses

### H1 — Relationship-plus-journey whitespace

A one-companion relationship inside a complete browser adventure about
restoration and moral choice may feel more distinctive than a collection-first
or care-loop-first proposition.

**Falsify when:** intended adults and players cannot accurately recall the
relationship, restoration, and choice after seeing authentic proof, or prefer a
simpler familiar category promise.

### H2 — Gameplay leads; AI explains

The emotional/playable promise should lead. AI and procedural systems should
follow as evidence for responsiveness and craft.

**Falsify when:** accurate AI-led language consistently improves qualified
comprehension and play intent without increasing misleading expectations or
trust concern.

### H3 — Adult-controlled discovery first

Initial discovery should target adults, guardians, creators, press, partners,
and general creature/narrative audiences while the product remains safe for a
younger core.

**Falsify when:** moderated evidence shows this framing makes the game unclear
or inaccessible to the intended player, or lawful non-behavioral alternatives
perform materially better.

### H4 — Trust is product evidence, not a slogan

Transparent limits, age boundaries, local-first design, and human governance
may strengthen permission and partnership decisions only when the underlying
controls are real and easy to inspect.

**Falsify when:** guardians and partners cannot find or understand the evidence,
or the trust layer does not address their actual concerns.

### H5 — Visible moments create the acquisition unit

Hatch/bond, restoration, and Project Beacon are likely to be stronger creator,
press, search, and social units than generic feature lists.

**Falsify when:** approved matched tests show those moments do not improve
qualified comprehension or meaningful play relative to factual text.

### H6 — Browser access is leverage only if reliability is proof

No-download, no-account starting access may reduce friction, but it becomes a
negative if boot, performance, save, input, audio, or mobile behavior is weak.

**Falsify when:** observed first sessions show browser convenience is not
noticed or technical friction dominates the proposition.

## Operating cadence

Run the radar monthly and at meaningful product/category events:

1. recheck first-party URLs and archive the observed date;
2. record launches, access/platform changes, positioning, pricing model,
   creator/community mechanics, AI claims, and material trust changes;
3. distinguish source fact, inference, unknown, and customer evidence;
4. update only hypotheses whose source or accepted evidence changed;
5. route material implications to A-019 experiments, A-021 search, A-026
   launch readiness, A-027 proof, and A-007 commercial evaluation;
6. require human review before any public comparison or strategic change.

## Current decision

Keep the working position: **browser creature-raising action-adventure with a
living companion and choice-driven science-fiction world**. Lead with the
relationship and journey, not “AI game.” This is a research hypothesis until
adult/guardian evidence and authentic gameplay proof exist. Do not use
“category-defining,” “first,” “only,” “market-leading,” or numerical market
claims.
