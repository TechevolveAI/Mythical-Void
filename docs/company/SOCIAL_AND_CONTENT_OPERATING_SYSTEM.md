# Social and Content Operating System

**Status:** Foundation; no social accounts connected and no publishing
authorized  
**Date:** 11 August 2026

## 1. Objective

Use content to make the product's living moments understandable, earn trust,
create qualified discovery, and learn—not to maximize posting volume.

The system optimizes for:

- accurate expectation before play;
- authentic product proof;
- meaningful play or useful conversation;
- audience and partner learning;
- long-term brand memory and search discoverability;
- low moderation and governance debt.

Followers, impressions, and agent output volume are diagnostic numbers, not the
company outcome.

## 4. Content lifecycle

```text
Approved objective
  -> source facts + proof objects
  -> channel brief
  -> channel-native draft
  -> deterministic claims/proof checks
  -> brand, safety, privacy, accessibility evaluation
  -> complete publication preview
  -> Kevin approval with expiry
  -> scoped publisher executes exactly once
  -> monitor replies/incidents/outcome
  -> record learning and archive
```

Every package carries claim IDs, proof IDs, destination, audience, alt text or
captions, generated-media status, disclosure requirements, measure, approval,
expiry, and external action ID.

Every package also names its intended reader: child, teenager, parent/guardian,
general player, creator, press, partner, or professional audience. The main
message must be understandable without technical knowledge. When technical
detail matters, give the plain meaning first and place the deeper explanation
after it or behind a clearly labelled link.

Changing the copy, media, account, audience, link, scheduled time, paid status,
or disclosure invalidates approval.

## 5. Publishing controls

### Agents may

- research public conversation and platform rules;
- propose briefs and calendars;
- draft and adapt approved-source content;
- validate claims, proof, links, accessibility, and duplication;
- assemble a complete approval package;
- compile aggregate results and reply themes.

### Agents may not without explicit approval

- create or claim an account;
- publish, schedule, delete, edit, boost, or pay for a post;
- reply, quote, mention, follow, direct-message, or join a group;
- accept platform terms or connect advertising/analytics pixels;
- contact a child or move a conversation to a private channel;
- imitate a person, fabricate community activity, or use engagement bots;
- manufacture testimonials, scarcity, controversy, or social proof.

## 6. Channel-native adaptation

Repurposing is not copying the same paragraph everywhere.

For each approved source moment:

- **website:** canonical detail, accessible media, durable context, accurate
  metadata;
- **short video:** one observable moment, on-screen context, captions, honest
  gameplay/illustration label, useful destination;
- **long video:** sequence, explanation, limitations, build/version context;
- **professional post:** company problem, evidence, decision, implementation,
  and learning;
- **community post:** discussion-relevant context and question, no disguised
  promotion;
- **creator/press package:** why it fits that audience, authentic build/proof,
  concise facts, access/support, and disclosure information.

The content validator rejects non-text packages without approved proof.

## 7. AI and generated-media disclosure

- Never label deterministic authored dialogue as live generative AI.
- Never call a creature sentient, conscious, truly alive, or capable of
  unrestricted memory.
- Label generated or materially generated imagery/video in the package and use
  platform disclosures where applicable.
- Do not imply a marketing illustration is gameplay.
- Keep the source build and edits available internally.
- Do not train on or reuse community/player submissions outside their recorded
  permission.
- Explain the player outcome and control, not merely the model/vendor.

## 8. Conversation and engagement tiers

| Tier | Example | Agent role | Send authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R0 Factual routine | Where to play, cost, supported format | Draft from claims/knowledge base | Human initially; bounded approval later |
| R1 Product conversation | Feature question, thoughtful feedback | Summarize and draft contextual reply | Human |
| R2 Criticism/misinformation | AI concern, safety concern, disputed behavior | Source evidence, surface uncertainty | Kevin/subject owner |
| R3 Restricted | Child contact, safeguarding, harassment, legal, security, press crisis | Stop ordinary automation and alert | Named human only |

Do not reward provocation with automated debate. Correct material facts once
with evidence, protect people, and disengage under the approved moderation rule.

## 9. Moderation foundation

Before comments or a community are actively promoted, define:

- public rules and age scope;
- moderator owner, backup, hours, and urgent channel;
- hide/delete/limit/block/escalate criteria;
- evidence preservation and privacy boundaries;
- appeal/correction path where appropriate;
- spam, impersonation, grooming, hate, sexual content, threats, self-harm,
  doxxing, scams, and malicious-link procedures;
- cross-platform incident coordination;
- wellbeing limits for the human moderator.

Disabling comments or delaying a community is acceptable when coverage does not
exist. Unmoderated reach is not growth.

## 10. Editorial cadence

Start milestone-led rather than daily:

- one canonical product/company update when something real changes;
- one to three channel-native proof pieces derived from it;
- one learning/trust follow-up if evidence warrants it;
- replies only within staffed coverage;
- a monthly content review that removes low-value cadence.

Do not start a recurring schedule until at least three approved proof objects
exist and the first research round clarifies audience/message.

## 11. Measurement

For each package select one primary outcome:

- qualified visit to a tagged canonical page;
- play selection or approved activation proxy;
- research signup through an approved adult/guardian flow;
- relevant creator/press/partner reply;
- accurate message recall in research;
- useful evidence or question theme;
- search visibility/backlink earned.

Pair with guardrails: negative feedback theme, misleading expectation,
safeguarding incident, moderation workload, unsubscribes/blocks, technical
failure, spend, and unsupported claim rate.

Platform impressions and likes are reported with context and never interpreted
as players, customers, or revenue.

## 14. Campaign atomization assurance

A-034 maintains the first source-bound campaign package in
[`content/campaigns/project-beacon-foundation.json`](content/campaigns/project-beacon-foundation.json).
Internal package readiness means only that canonical copy, source claims,
literal excerpts, links, accessibility, disclosures, and authority state are
coherent. It does not approve copy or authorize measurement, scheduling,
publication, replies, promotion, or any other external action.

Every later atomized package must preserve the same source-to-claim chain. A
variant that adds an unsupported claim, testimonial, asset, account identity,
tracking parameter, destination, or stronger qualifier fails before review.
