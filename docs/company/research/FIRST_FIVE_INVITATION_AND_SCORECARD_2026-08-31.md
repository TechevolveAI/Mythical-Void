# First Five invitation and scorecard

**Status:** ready for Kevin to approve; nobody has been contacted

This is the complete founder action for the first five unfamiliar adult
players. It is intentionally small. We learn whether the game makes sense
before asking a platform or a large audience to pay attention.

## The exact invitation

Kevin sends this only to adults aged 18 or over whom he already has a
legitimate way to contact:

> Hi — I’m testing a free browser game that started as a father-and-son project.
> Would you be willing to try it for about twelve minutes while I
> watch quietly? We are testing the game, not you. Nothing is recorded, I will
> not write down your name or anything personal, and you can stop at any time.
> If you are happy to help, we can arrange a time that suits you.

Do not add a claim about AI, NASA, uniqueness, popularity or educational
benefit to this invitation. Do not send a reminder if somebody does not reply.
Do not invite children.

## At the start of the session

Open `https://mythicalvoid.com/` in a fresh private browser window. Say:

> Please use the game as you normally would. I will stay quiet unless you ask
> for help. You can stop whenever you want. We are testing the game, not you.

Do not explain what to press, what should hatch or what the first goal is.

## Stop immediately if

- a participant turns out to be under 18;
- the game repeatedly fails to load;
- the creature is missing, clipped or covered by a block;
- phone controls cannot be used;
- a participant raises a serious safety or trust concern.

The invitation remains unapproved until Kevin explicitly says to begin. This
document does not authorise contact, recording, compensation, publication or
platform submission.
