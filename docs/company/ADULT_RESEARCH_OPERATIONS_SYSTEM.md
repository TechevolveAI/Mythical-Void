# Adult Research Operations System

**Status:** Internal Round 001A package ready; recruitment and collection gated  
**Date:** 11 August 2026  
**Control:** A-029  
**Machine-readable source:** [`research/round-001a-operations.json`](research/round-001a-operations.json)

## Outcome

Round 001A will produce Mythical's first accepted customer evidence about
positioning, guardian trust, and the first playable value. It is a purposeful
qualitative study with ten consenting adults:

- six parents or guardians;
- four adult creature, cozy, or narrative-game players;
- a balanced 4/3/3 assignment across the current, creature-relationship-led, and
  story-led message cards.

It does not estimate market size, prevalence, retention, or a child audience.
No participant is a lead, subscriber, marketing contact, testimonial, or user
profile.

## Operating boundary

A-029 prepares and validates the study package. It cannot recruit, schedule,
contact, record, compensate, collect, moderate, import evidence, follow up, or
change positioning. Those activities require named humans, restricted systems,
purpose-specific participant information and consent, and separately approved
execution.

Round 001A is adult-only. Do not collect a child's name, exact age, birth date,
school, location, contact details, account, image, voice, health information,
or gameplay identifier. A guardian may discuss their decision process in
general terms; the moderator redirects unnecessary information.

## Recruitment screener

The human research owner asks only what is needed to assign the adult sample:

1. Are you 18 or older? (`yes` required; do not record exact age.)
2. Which best describes you?
   - parent/guardian who helps decide what a younger person may play;
   - adult who plays creature, cozy, or narrative-adventure games;
   - neither.
3. Before today, had you seen or worked on Mythical Void? (`yes` excludes the
   main sample but may be recorded as a separate pilot.)
4. Which device do you usually use for browser games? (`mobile`, `desktop`,
   `both`, `other`, `none`.)
5. Would you like to request an accessibility accommodation? (`yes/no`; details
   go only to the restricted human scheduling route.)

Do not ask for a child's details, household income, ethnicity, disability,
precise location, marketing preferences, or unrelated personal history.

## Consent confirmation

Immediately before the session, the human moderator confirms and records in
the restricted study system:

- participant is 18 or older;
- participant received and understood the approved information;
- participation and note-taking are voluntary;
- recording remains off, unless a separately reviewed optional recording
  consent is introduced later;
- participant may skip or stop;
- research permission is purpose-specific and not reusable for marketing;
- participant knows the approved contact, withdrawal deadline, compensation,
  retention, deletion, and complaint route.

Silence, attendance, or completion does not count as consent.

## Moderator session

Use the existing 45–55 minute
[`ROUND_001_POSITIONING_AND_TRUST.md`](research/ROUND_001_POSITIONING_AND_TRUST.md)
script. Before admitting a participant, the moderator checks:

- assigned anonymous session code and message card;
- exact stable product build and site/release label;
- consent state; notes-only recording state; no personal desktop notifications;
- accessibility accommodation handled through the restricted route;
- stop/escalation route available;
- no unrelated observer, AI meeting bot, transcription service, analytics,
  CRM, or marketing pixel connected.

The moderator records observable behavior before interpretation and does not
correct comprehension tasks until each task ends.

## Stop and escalation rules

Stop ordinary research and follow the approved human route if the session
reveals immediate danger, abuse/exploitation, self-harm, illegal or sexual
content, a privacy/security incident, a legal or data-rights request, unexpected
child participation, or contact data outside the restricted system. Do not
copy the content into shared notes or ask an agent to resolve it.

For ordinary withdrawal, stop the session, record only the minimum withdrawal
state, suppress follow-up, and apply the approved deletion rule. Compensation
must not depend on positive feedback or completion after a voluntary stop.

## Evidence transformation

After each session, two layers remain separate:

1. **Restricted operations record:** contact, consent, scheduling,
   compensation, accommodation, withdrawal, and any raw notes allowed by the
   approved retention plan.
2. **Shared evidence candidates:** short de-identified observations formatted
   for `customer/evidence.json`, with interpretation, alternatives, confidence,
   product version, protocol, owner, decision, retention date, and a restricted
   pointer—never contact or transcript data.

A human reviewer compares each candidate with the restricted source before
`status: accepted`. A-005 may synthesize only accepted records. One session may
produce multiple observations; session count is not market prevalence.

## Decision outputs

After all ten sessions—or an earlier safety/invalid-study stop—the named owner
records:

- which message expectations were accurate or misleading;
- trust-critical misunderstandings, irrespective of frequency;
- first-session blockers and visible value moments;
- contradictory evidence and sample limitations;
- disposition of RH-001 through RH-004 and relevant MH-001 through MH-006;
- recommendation or no-decision for D-002;
- evidence-backed Game Development handoffs;
- what participants may later be told changed, within their approved contact
  permission.
