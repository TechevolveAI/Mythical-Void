# Gameplay Proof Production System

**Status:** Production specifications ready; capture and publication gated  
**Date:** 11 August 2026  
**Control:** A-027  
**Machine-readable source:** [`content/proof-production.json`](content/proof-production.json)

## Outcome

Mythical needs three pieces of authentic gameplay evidence before it can
credibly explain its companion, world, or central choice. A-027 converts that
need into exact production briefs for:

1. hatch through a concrete first bond response;
2. player-led realm restoration;
3. a Project Beacon discovery or choice without ending spoilers.

The system is deliberately separate from publication. Game Development owns
the stable build and capture execution. The company proof owner defines the
evidentiary requirement. A separate reviewer accepts provenance, rights,
claims, accessibility, edits, and representation. Kevin approves any later
public package through the applicable channel and external-action controls.

## Truth before polish

Every proof must preserve an observable chain:

```text
known starting state
  -> visible player action
  -> visible game response
  -> enough aftermath to understand the result
```

A montage may be derived only after the continuous master exists. Editing may
remove waiting time, resize, caption, normalize audio, or mask an accidental
identifier, but every cut and mask must be recorded. It may not insert a
different outcome, reorder cause and effect, hide a failure that changes the
claim, present concept art as gameplay, or add generated pixels, voice, or
animation to a gameplay proof.

## Required production record

Each capture records:

- exact commit, build identifier, build digest, release label, and capture date;
- proof owner, capture operator, independent reviewer, and approval date;
- platform, viewport, input method, graphics/audio settings, save fixture, and
  whether network-dependent features were disabled;
- raw master path/digest/duration and every derived asset path/digest;
- the visible starting state, player action, response, and aftermath;
- claim IDs and support level, plus explicit non-claims;
- first-party rights, third-party asset/audio review, generated-media status,
  privacy screen, edit decision list, captions, alt text, and expiry trigger.

No player, child, account, email, entered creature name, desktop notification,
file path, debug secret, token, or personal save may appear. Use a clean,
company-controlled fixture. Capture from a dirty or unidentified build cannot
be approved.

## Acceptance boundaries

### PF-003 — Hatch and first bond

The continuous master begins before the committed hatch action and ends after
one concrete companion response. Procedural appearance may support CL-005 only
as a visible example, never as proof of global uniqueness. CL-006 requires a
specific implemented memory or remembered state to be named and shown; an
expressive animation alone does not prove memory or intelligence.

### PF-004 — Realm restoration

The master shows a legible damaged state, the player's relevant action, the
restoration transition, and a stable restored state. One realm demonstrates
the restoration mechanic but does not alone prove that all six realms are
complete; CL-004 still requires a separate build check.

### PF-005 — Project Beacon choice

The master establishes enough fictional context to understand the discovery
or choice and shows the player action and immediate response. It must avoid the
ending and cannot imply branching consequences unless the captured sequence
actually demonstrates them. CL-008 remains a fiction/world claim.

## Output set

For each proof, preserve one 16:9 continuous master with game audio, a captioned
review copy, one accessible still, and—only after acceptance—optional
channel-native derivatives. Vertical crops must keep the player action and game
response visible. Music replacement requires separately recorded rights and
cannot conceal important gameplay audio.
