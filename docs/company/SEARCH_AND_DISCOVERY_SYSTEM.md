## 1. Objective

Make Mythical Void findable when someone already knows the name and when an
eligible player, guardian, creator, partner, or journalist searches for the
kind of experience Mythical genuinely provides.

Search work must improve truthful comprehension and meaningful play—not create
thin pages, manufacture authority, or chase traffic unrelated to the product.

The machine-readable opportunity map is
[`search/search-opportunities.json`](search/search-opportunities.json). A-021
validates its claims, proof, target paths, sources, dependencies, and external

## 2. Original 11 August evidence

On 11 August 2026, a directional web-search sample used eight branded,
domain, and category queries. The sampled `site:mythicalvoid.com`, exact-name,
domain, and Project Beacon queries did not surface a Mythical result. This is
not a complete index census or ranking claim; it is consistent with A-001's
live finding that `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, and `/llms.txt` return the SPA
HTML shell and the live homepage lacks the prepared structured metadata.

Adjacent first-party product pages surfaced with direct category language:

- [Dobu Labs](https://dobudob.com/) describes a virtual-lab species and AI
  overseer;
- [Beanlings browser pet game](https://beanlings.com/s/browser-pet-game)
  explicitly leads with a free browser pet, growth stages, DNA, and no
  download;
- [Browmi](https://browmi.sylphe.cc/) uses “virtual pet browser extension,”
  “cozy browser game,” “procedural creature,” and “creature-raising” language;
- [CipherBound](https://cipherbound.com/) identifies itself as a
  browser-based creature-battling RPG.

These are discovery-pattern observations, not endorsements, market-share
evidence, or permission to copy positioning. Mythical's strongest truthful
space appears to be the combination of immediate browser access, companion
bond, restoration adventure, Project Beacon choice, and responsible operating
boundaries. Authentic gameplay proof is still required before broad category
content.

## 3. Search architecture

```text
Branded truth
  -> canonical home / play / privacy / terms
  -> explicit category and access language
  -> authentic companion / restoration / choice proof
  -> parent and guardian trust explanation
  -> bounded craft / procedural / AI explanation
  -> evidence-led updates and earned references
```

The initial query clusters are:

1. **Brand and navigation** — help people find the canonical game and policy
   pages.
2. **Browser creature adventure** — explain the actual playable category and
   immediate-access model.
3. **Companion bond and genetics** — show concrete hatch, bond, memory, and
   procedural variation without absolute uniqueness claims.
4. **Living world and choice** — demonstrate restoration and Project Beacon
   through real gameplay.
5. **Parent and guardian trust** — answer cost, account, saves, combat, AI,
   privacy, and contact questions without claiming a rating or legal guarantee.
6. **Responsible AI and game craft** — explain actual systems, providers,
   fallbacks, authored/generated boundaries, and governance after provider
   policy reconciliation.

## 4. Page rules

Every indexable page must have:

- one audience and search intent;
- a useful purpose independent of ranking;
- canonical URL, unique title, useful description, one clear heading, and
  crawlable body copy;
- only registered usable or qualified claims;
- proof for visual, gameplay, memory, AI, animation, or outcome assertions;
- accessible media, captions/alt text, provenance, and source build where
  applicable;
- links to relevant play, privacy, terms, parent, and contact information;
- no fabricated reviews, FAQ answers, dates, authorship, structured data,
  ratings, prices, availability, or organizational identity;
- explicit review and expiry triggers.

Do not create doorway pages for small wording variants. Consolidate overlapping
intent into the most useful canonical page.

## 5. Agent role

Agents may:

- sample current search results and official first-party pages;
- maintain query clusters, content gaps, sources, and change alerts;
- draft page briefs from approved claims and proof;
- validate titles, descriptions, headings, canonicals, links, accessibility,
  structured data, duplication, and unsupported claims;
- prepare an exact preview and search-submission package;
- compare post-release technical and aggregate discovery outcomes.

Agents may not:

- publish pages, submit sitemaps, change webmaster settings, buy links, run paid
  search, create accounts, or contact sites without approval;
- claim a ranking or indexing state from a small search sample;
- mass-produce near-duplicate pages or hide generated filler;
- scrape personal data or target children behaviorally;
- use competitor trademarks deceptively or imply affiliation;
- publish a page whose proof, provider, privacy, identity, or release dependency
  is unresolved.

## 6. Measurement

Initial measures:

- A-001 technical finding count;
- verified indexing of canonical URLs in an approved webmaster source;
- branded query result presence across a documented sample;
- accurate message recall in adult research;
- approved aggregate play-selection and activation attempts when M1 exists;
- qualified creator, press, partner, or community references;
- unsupported-claim, wrong-expectation, safeguarding, and moderation outcomes.

Search impressions, average position, and clicks require verified Search
Console or equivalent access and must retain query/sample limitations. They do
not prove players, customers, retention, or revenue.
