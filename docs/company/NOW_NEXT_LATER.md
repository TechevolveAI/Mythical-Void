# Mythical: what happens next

This is the short, plain-English view of the company plan. The longer files in
this folder are working notes for the people and tools who will run it.

## Now — build trust before we grow

- Keep the website accurate, welcoming, and easy for a young player and their
  parent to understand.
- Show the real game: the companion, the six worlds, the damaged guardians,
  Phaser 3, the genetics system, optional AI story moments, and NASA learning
  moments.
- Collect no public messages until a real Mythical inbox and a named adult
  owner exist.
- Let helpful software prepare checks, summaries, and drafts, while Kevin
  approves anything that leaves the company.

## Next — open the company doors

- Add Mythical addresses inside the existing Google Workspace. First decide
  whether they should be shared addresses or separate inboxes; a second
  Workspace subscription is not assumed.
- Create the official social accounts and reserve the Mythical name.
- Turn on simple, privacy-respecting website measurement.
- Give parents and players a clear way to send feedback, with safeguarding
  handled by a real adult.

## Later — grow carefully

- Turn approved game facts into helpful posts, videos, school-friendly space
  activities, and launch updates.
- Learn what players find exciting or confusing without collecting more personal
  information than we need.
- Explore partnerships, sales, and funding only when the game and support
  experience are ready.

The rule is simple: software may prepare and organise the work; people remain
responsible for safety, truth, money, and public promises.
