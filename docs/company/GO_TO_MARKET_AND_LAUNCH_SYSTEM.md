# Go-to-Market and Launch System

**Status:** Foundation implemented; all outward launch waves gated  
**Date:** 11 August 2026  
**Machine-readable source:** [`growth/launch-readiness.json`](growth/launch-readiness.json)

## Purpose

Mythical should grow because real players can understand, trust, enjoy, and
recommend a distinctive game—not because an autonomous system can create more
noise. This system turns positioning, product proof, discovery, research,
engagement, distribution, measurement, finance, and governance into one staged
launch path.

A-026 is an internal assurance workflow. It can assemble evidence and expose
the next gate. It cannot recruit, publish, contact, deploy, submit to a search
engine or portal, collect data, target a person, spend money, or authorize a
launch.

## The launch thesis

Lead with the emotional and playable truth:

> A one-of-one alien companion, a living world to restore, and choices that
> change what getting home means.

Use AI, procedural generation, and animation as supported craft evidence—not
as a substitute for the player promise or as an unsupported claim of
intelligence. Keep the product safe for younger players while directing initial
research, discovery, creator, press, and commercial activity to adults and
guardians. Behavioral targeting toward children is prohibited.

## Launch ladder

| Wave | Outcome | Entry gate | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Quiet owned access | Preserve the existing playable route without promotion | Current production access only | Known constraints remain explicit; no growth claim |
| 2. Controlled adult research | Learn audience language, trust concerns, and first-session breaks | Named research and safeguarding owners; approved adult route | Accepted, de-identified evidence with uncertainty and contradictions |
| 3. Owned discovery release | Make the canonical site technically findable and trustworthy | Isolated release, rollback, owner, approval, and post-release checks | A-001 has no major findings and owned pages are verified |
| 4. Evidence-led creator/press pilot | Test whether authentic game moments earn qualified attention | Stable build, approved gameplay proofs, creator/support kit, exact outreach approval | Small reconciled pilot with play and qualitative evidence |
| 5. Portal/distribution pilot | Validate one technically and commercially suitable distribution route | Portable-build evidence, rights/privacy review, named owner, approved platform action | One bounded pilot with technical, audience, cost, and support evidence |
| 6. Broader public launch | Prove a repeatable awareness-to-meaningful-play path | Prior waves reviewed; support, incident, rollback, measurement, and capacity ready | Activation, trust, reliability, return, and cost evidence inside guardrails |
| 7. Paid acquisition/monetization | Test durable economics without harming trust | Verified financial baseline, unit costs, lawful measurement, pricing/legal review, strict caps | Cohort and unit-economics evidence; no child behavioral targeting or dark patterns |

The waves are ordered learning boundaries, not a calendar. A later wave cannot
be ready while an earlier required wave is unready. Kevin's decision is
necessary where named, but a conversational response is never itself an
execution token.

## Eight readiness tracks

1. **Trust, governance, and safeguarding** — named accountable humans,
   restricted-case routes, privacy boundaries, and approval trust.
2. **Product and release** — stable build, isolated scope, rollback, release
   owner, technical health, and source-to-deploy evidence.
3. **Audience and positioning** — adult research, accepted evidence, audience
   choice, comprehension, disconfirming evidence, and explicit uncertainty.
4. **Proof and creative** — authentic hatch/bond, restoration, and Project
   Beacon footage with provenance, rights, accessibility, and accurate claims.
5. **Owned discovery and channels** — crawlability, canonical pages, verified
   accounts, named owners, moderation, measurement, and exact publication
   approval.
6. **Distribution and partnerships** — technical fit, rights, economics,
   integration boundary, support obligations, and approved contact/action.
7. **Engagement and support** — adult/guardian consent where required,
   suppression, safeguarding, service ownership, escalation, and close-loop.
8. **Measurement and finance** — lawful minimal signals, known event
   boundaries, reconciled financial truth, cost caps, stop rules, and runway
   method.

Each track is evidence-backed in the launch-readiness file. `ready: true` is
invalid unless its listed prerequisites are satisfied in their authoritative
registers.

## Operating loop

1. A-026 reads the authoritative company registers and calculates launch
   blockers.
2. AG-002 may recommend only the next admissible learning wave; AG-010 remains
   the independent evaluator.
3. Internal preparation can continue through the objective queue and A-024;
   A-034 may produce source-bound campaign variants for review without granting
   a channel identity or publication authority.
4. A consequential external step requires a scoped A-011 envelope, named human
   approval, a separate executor, expiry, limits, rollback, and reconciliation.
5. After a wave, accepted evidence updates the registers before the next wave
   can be assessed.

## Success measures

Use a balanced launch view: comprehension, meaningful play, trust, technical
health, return where lawfully measurable, qualitative evidence, support load,
guardrail outcomes, and cost. Impressions, generated posts, simulated personas,
or agent activity do not establish market demand.
