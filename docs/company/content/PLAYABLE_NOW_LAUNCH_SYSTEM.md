# Mythical Void: Playable Now launch system

**State:** internally ready; social accounts and publishing still require Kevin  
**Purpose:** help adults, families, players, educators, creators and the games industry discover that Mythical Void can be played now.

## The simple message

> A universe of creatures is waiting. Mythical Void is a free browser adventure with creature genetics, six living realms, real choices and optional NASA-powered discovery. No download or account is needed to start.

The link is always: <https://mythicalvoid.com/>

Do not use “AI companion”, promise that every generated creature is globally unique, or present generated marketing artwork as gameplay.

## Who we speak to first

1. **Parents and guardians** — a free, accessible adventure with clear safety information and no account required to start.
2. **Adult and teenage players** — strange creatures, platforming, restoration, exploration and choices.
3. **Indie-game and creative-technology people** — a father-and-son experiment growing into an AI-first Irish studio.
4. **Educators and STEM communities** — optional NASA data gives the fictional universe a doorway into real space discovery.
5. **Creators and press** — an unusual playable story with strong visual variety and a genuine founder origin.

This is not child-targeted behavioural advertising. Posts should be understandable to younger players while account ownership, replies and moderation remain adult-run.

## The first four weeks

The exact ready-to-run sequence, approved assets and approval gates now live in [`channel-launch/FOUR_WEEK_LAUNCH_CALENDAR.json`](channel-launch/FOUR_WEEK_LAUNCH_CALENDAR.json). Week 1 starts only after Kevin records an official channel link, so preparation can continue without pretending an account exists.

### Week 1 — it is playable

- Publish the short launch announcement.
- Publish the father-and-son origin story on a professional channel.
- Publish one short visual showing the breadth of the creature universe. Label the hero artwork as an AI-generated marketing illustration inspired by real genetics outputs, not gameplay.

### Week 2 — prove the game

- Show authentic gameplay from the crash site to first creature encounter.
- Show one restoration battle with the corruption clearly identified as the target.
- Invite adults and guardians to play and describe one confusing or delightful moment once the feedback channel exists.

### Week 3 — explain what is different

- Explain the creature genetics in plain language.
- Show how NASA's public data can appear as optional space-discovery moments.
- Explain where generative AI helps and where people remain responsible.

### Week 4 — learn and repeat

- Share one real improvement only after accepted player evidence exists.
- Review which posts led to qualified website visits and play selections.
- Continue only the formats that produced useful discovery without creating unsafe moderation work.

## Recommended channel order

1. **LinkedIn company page** for the founder story, studio progress and responsible-AI practice.
2. **YouTube** for real gameplay, a short trailer and durable search discovery.
3. **One short-video channel** only after its account owner, backup, comment rules and moderation coverage are ready.
4. **Relevant communities** through human participation that follows each community's rules; never drop promotional links without context.
5. **Discord and email** remain deferred until safeguarding, consent and support coverage exist.

## The minimum Kevin setup

The company can prepare everything else. Kevin is needed to:

- choose and create the first official social account;
- record the exact account link and recovery owner;
- switch on multi-factor authentication;
- approve the finished post and image together;
- name when comments will be checked and who handles anything sensitive.

No paid advertising is needed for the first campaign. The aim is to learn which story earns genuine play, not to buy attention before the message is proven.

## Weekly operating rhythm

**Monday:** select one true story and its proof.  
**Tuesday:** prepare one main post and useful versions.  
**Wednesday:** Kevin reviews the full preview.  
**Thursday:** publish only the approved version while comment coverage is available.  
**Friday:** record useful questions, visits and play selections; do not treat likes as players.

The studio does not need to appear busy every day. It needs to be interesting, truthful and available when people respond.
