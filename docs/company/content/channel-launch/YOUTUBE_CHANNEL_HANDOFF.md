# Open the Mythical Void YouTube channel

This is now a short account-creation job. The artwork, wording and safety settings are prepared. It does **not** need another Google Workspace subscription: the existing Google account can create a separate business-name YouTube channel.

## Before you begin

Look at the finished banner and profile image together. If either one feels wrong, stop there and tell the studio what to change. Nothing has been uploaded.

- Profile image: [`youtube-profile-v1.png`](../../../../public/marketing/channel-kit/youtube/youtube-profile-v1.png)
- Channel banner: [`youtube-channel-banner-v1.jpg`](../../../../public/marketing/channel-kit/youtube/youtube-channel-banner-v1.jpg)

The banner is generated marketing artwork, not a game screenshot. It uses more alien forms—radial, liquid, mineral, gaseous and folded-dimensional life—and keeps the approved Mythical Void emblem in the centre.

## Create the channel

1. Sign in to YouTube with the existing Google account Kevin wants to keep as owner.
2. Create a new channel with a business or other name.
3. Use **Mythical Void** as the channel name.
4. Try these handles in order: `@MythicalVoid`, `@PlayMythicalVoid`, `@MythicalVoidGame`. Availability has not been checked or promised.
5. Upload the prepared profile image and banner.
6. Paste this description:

> Enter the Void. Hatch a strange creature, explore six living realms and decide what Project Beacon becomes. Mythical Void is a free browser adventure made by an independent Irish studio that began as a father-and-son experiment with imagination and generative AI. Play now with no download or account needed. Generated artwork is labelled; gameplay videos show the real game. https://mythicalvoid.com/

7. Add these links in this order:
   - Play: `https://mythicalvoid.com/`
   - The story: `https://mythicalvoid.com/story/`
   - Press and creators: `https://mythicalvoid.com/press/`
8. In YouTube Studio, choose to review the audience setting for every upload.
9. Keep comments off. They can move to **Hold all** only after a named adult and backup have an agreed review time.
10. Turn on multi-factor authentication. Never share the Google password. Add future helpers through YouTube Studio permissions with the smallest role they need.
11. Send the exact public channel link back to the studio register. Do not upload a video yet.

## What the studio does next

Once the link is recorded, the website can point to the real channel. Kevin then reviews the exact first video, thumbnail, title, description, audience choice and comment setting together. The finished trailer remains held back until that review is complete.

No channel creation, upload, comment opening, public reply or paid promotion has been carried out by this kit.
