# Mythical Void's first outreach wave

The first three introductions are researched and written. Nothing has been sent.

## The order

1. **Imirt** — the strongest home-market fit. The message presents Mythical Void as a playable Irish game that began with a father and his nine-year-old son.
2. **Alpha Beta Gamer** — the strongest immediate player-discovery fit. The message leads with the free working browser game and real gameplay, not a big promise.
3. **Phaser** — the strongest game-development fit. The message asks whether a substantial game made with Phaser belongs in its showcase.

The finished subjects and message bodies are in [FIRST_CREATOR_OUTREACH_WAVE.json](FIRST_CREATOR_OUTREACH_WAVE.json).

## What Kevin needs to do later

An extra Google Workspace subscription is not required for this work to continue. When Kevin is ready to send the first message, he can either:

- use a future Mythical Void address; or
- approve one message being sent from his existing professional address, clearly signed as Kevin from Mythical Void.

Before each message goes anywhere, Kevin approves that named recipient and the final wording. The messages go one at a time, with no attachments and no bulk mailing.

## What we are deliberately not doing yet

- **Irish Tech News:** prepare a useful founder story, not a generic press release.
- **Technology.ie:** wait for a clear launch or player milestone.
- **Wholesome Games:** watch for a genuinely relevant open submission; do not force a cosy label.
- **Overlooked Games:** wait until Mythical Void has a public store page.
- **Games for Change:** watch a future cycle. The 2026 deadline has passed, entry costs money, and a strong submission needs real learning aims and evidence.
- **IndieGameInn:** wait for a polished human-commentary gameplay video.
- **Indie Games Developer:** wait for the authentic gameplay trailer.

## The rules that stay in place

- Call the beings **creatures**.
- Never claim that every creature is unique.
- Never present generated artwork as gameplay.
- Say that the game uses NASA public data; never imply NASA endorsement.
- Do not reveal the child's name, image, school, location, routine or private accounts.
- Send at most one follow-up after seven days, then stop.
