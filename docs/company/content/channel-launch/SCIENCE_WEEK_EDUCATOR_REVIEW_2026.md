# Adult educator review - Water That Isn't Water

This is an internal review sheet for the three-page Mythical Void activity pack. It is not a child test, public survey, event application or request for endorsement.

## Before reviewing

- Review the pack yourself as an adult. Do not involve children at this stage.
- Use a simple local code such as `R01`; do not record your name or contact details in this file.
- Check the printed A4 pages as well as the screen version.
- Stop if you find unsafe materials, direct child contact, personal-data collection, a misleading science claim or confusion about endorsement.
- A positive review does not approve publication. Kevin still makes that decision.

## Rating

- **1 - Stop:** unsafe, misleading or unusable.
- **2 - Major change needed:** the activity should not move forward in its current form.
- **3 - Minor change needed:** sound overall, with specific improvements required.
- **4 - Clear:** ready for the next review gate.
