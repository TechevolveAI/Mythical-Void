# Water That Isn't Water

## A possible adult-led Mythical Void activity for Science Week 2026

Science Week Ireland’s published theme for 8-15 November 2026 is **Water**. Mythical Void already contains a real playable realm called Stellar Reef, described in the game as a place where cosmic energy flows like water. That creates a strong bridge between real ocean-world science and a genuinely strange fictional organism.

The activity begins with three different ideas:

- Earth has liquid-water oceans.
- Scientists think Europa has a salty liquid-water ocean beneath its icy crust.
- Titan has rivers, lakes and seas of liquid methane and ethane on its surface.

Then the group crosses clearly into fiction: what could survive in Stellar Reef if cosmic energy flowed like water?

This is not an animal-drawing exercise. Designers begin with the environment and a survival problem. Their organism may be radial, liquid, distributed, crystalline, temporary, field-like or able to change state. It does not need a face, head, limbs, skin or one permanent body.

Each designer explains:

1. how the organism holds itself together;
2. how it senses a change in the flow;
3. where its energy comes from;
4. how it moves or spreads;
5. one prediction that could be tested inside the imagined rules;
6. which part of the damaged reef should be restored first.

The design must label three things separately: real evidence, scientific inference and fictional invention.
