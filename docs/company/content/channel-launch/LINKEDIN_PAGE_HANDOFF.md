# Open the Mythical Void LinkedIn Company Page

LinkedIn says a Company Page can be created for free from an existing personal profile. The Page remains separate from Kevin’s personal profile and has no separate username or shared password.

## Before you begin

Review the finished logo and cover together. If either one feels wrong, stop and tell the studio what to change. Nothing has been uploaded or published.

- Page logo: [`linkedin-page-logo-v1.png`](../../../../public/marketing/channel-kit/linkedin/linkedin-page-logo-v1.png)
- Page cover: [`linkedin-page-cover-v1.jpg`](../../../../public/marketing/channel-kit/linkedin/linkedin-page-cover-v1.jpg)

The cover uses generated Mythical Void marketing artwork, not a game screenshot. Important wording sits in the centre because LinkedIn can crop the edges on different screens.

## Create the Page

1. Sign in to Kevin’s real LinkedIn profile on desktop or iPhone. LinkedIn currently does not support Page creation on Android.
2. Choose **For Business → Create a Company Page → Company**.
3. Enter these details:
   - Name: **Mythical Void**
   - Public address: try `mythical-void`, then `play-mythical-void`. Availability has not been checked or promised.
   - Website: `https://mythicalvoid.com/`
   - Industry: **Computer Games**
   - Company size: choose the smallest truthful option
   - Company type: choose **Privately Held** only if it is legally accurate; otherwise use the closest truthful choice
4. Paste this tagline:

> An independent Irish studio building a browser creature adventure with imagination, responsible AI and real space discovery.

5. Confirm that Kevin has the right to act for Mythical Void and create the Page.
6. Upload the prepared 400 × 400 logo and 4200 × 700 cover.
7. Paste this About section:

> Mythical Void began at home with a father, his nine-year-old son and a question: what could generative AI tools and a lot of imagination make possible?
>
> Those conversations grew into strange creatures, living realms and a free browser adventure. Players hatch a creature, restore worlds affected by the Void and make choices about Project Beacon. Optional NASA-powered discoveries connect the fictional journey to real space.
>
> We are building the studio in the same spirit: small, independent, ambitious and AI-first, while people remain responsible for the story, safety, evidence and important decisions.
>
> Play now: https://mythicalvoid.com/
> The story: https://mythicalvoid.com/story/
> Press room: https://mythicalvoid.com/press/

8. Do not turn on Page messaging, create a post, invite followers, boost content or add paid-media access yet.
9. Turn on multi-factor authentication for Kevin’s personal LinkedIn account. Never share its password.
10. Send the exact public Page link back to the studio register and stop there.

## What the studio does next

The studio records the real link, checks the public Page and connects it from the website. Kevin then reviews the first post’s wording, image, description and timing together. Six posts are prepared, but none is automatically released.

Once a trusted backup adult exists, Kevin can add them through a Page role. Use **Content admin** for someone who needs to publish, or **Analyst** for someone who only needs to view performance. Keep **Super admin** limited to people who genuinely need complete control.

No Page creation, artwork upload, follower invitation, post, comment reply, message or paid promotion has been carried out by this kit.
