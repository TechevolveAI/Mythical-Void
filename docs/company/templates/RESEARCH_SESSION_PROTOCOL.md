## Before recruitment

- Define the hypothesis and what would change the decision.
- Complete privacy/safeguarding review appropriate to the audience.
- Define participant and guardian information/consent/assent where applicable.
- Collect only the minimum scheduling information in a restricted system.
- Define recording policy; default to no recording when observation notes can
  answer the question.
- Define compensation, withdrawal, retention, deletion, and incident handling.
- Test accessibility and the moderator emergency/stop procedure.

## Moderator opening

- Explain that the game is being tested, not the participant.
- Participation is voluntary and can stop without giving a reason.
- Ask permission before each recording or new data use.
- Tell the participant what the moderator can and cannot keep confidential.
- Do not ask for private identifiers or unrelated personal experiences.

## Observation tasks

For each task record observable behavior before interpretation.

Suggested first study:

1. Explain what game you expect from the storefront.
2. Find cost, account, privacy, AI, combat, and parent information.
3. Start the game and reach the first companion moment.
4. Explain what happened and what you think the companion understood.
5. State what you want to do next and any concern that would stop you.

## Interview prompts

- What did you think was happening?
- What made you feel that?
- What did you expect to happen next?
- What felt confusing, uncomfortable, exciting, or memorable?
- If you stopped here, what would be the reason?

Avoid leading questions such as “Did the creature feel intelligent?” Ask for
the participant's description first.

## After the session

- Separate observations, participant words, and researcher interpretation.
- De-identify the evidence record.
- Remove scheduling/contact data when no longer required.
- Route safeguarding, privacy, or security content outside the shared notes.
- Record evidence limits and contradictory observations.
- Link accepted evidence to a decision, experiment, or product handoff.
- Follow up with the participant only under the agreed contact permission.
