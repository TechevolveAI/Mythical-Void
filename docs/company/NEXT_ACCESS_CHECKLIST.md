# Next access checklist

The company system is ready for real operating channels, but Mythical does not yet have those channels. The first phase is therefore **channel creation**, followed by careful connection and testing.

Nothing below assumes that an inbox, social account, analytics property, or partner database already exists.

## In plain English

The website and the company plan are ready. The only things missing are the
real accounts and the people responsible for them. We will add them one at a
time, test each one safely, and keep Kevin approving anything that goes out to
the public.

## 1. Create the support routes

Needed: decide whether `hello@mythicalvoid.com` and `parents@mythicalvoid.com` should be aliases, group addresses, or separate mailboxes inside an existing Google Workspace. Do not assume a second Workspace subscription is needed. Also decide the recovery method and name the person who handles safeguarding cases.

Enables: sorting incoming questions, preparing grounded draft replies, and highlighting urgent or child-safety concerns.

Not enabled by default: sending replies, contacting a child directly, storing raw messages forever, or using support messages for marketing.

## 2. Create the official social accounts

Needed: choose the first channels, create them under the Mythical identity, reserve matching names, and name the person who approves posts.

The exact no-extra-mailbox setup fields for LinkedIn and YouTube are ready in
[`content/channel-launch/CHANNEL_OPENING_KIT_2026-08-27.md`](content/channel-launch/CHANNEL_OPENING_KIT_2026-08-27.md).
LinkedIn can use Kevin's existing real profile to administer a free company
Page. YouTube can use a Google Account Kevin already controls and a separate
public Mythical Void channel name, provided YouTube is enabled for that
account. Neither step requires another paid Google Workspace subscription.

Enables: preparing channel-specific drafts, scheduling suggestions, and a review queue based on approved claims and real proof.

Not enabled by default: publishing, replying to people, direct messaging, paid promotion, or creating new accounts.

## 3. Create the measurement property

Needed: create or confirm the Google property, record the retention choice, and name the person who can remove the tag.

Enables: reading simple public-page visit totals after consent.

Not enabled by default: game measurement, advertising profiles, child profiling, user IDs, or importing personal data.

## 4. Create the commercial and partner workspace

Needed: choose a private workspace, define the approved partner/customer type, set contact rules, and require Kevin’s approval for any outreach.

Enables: research, a sourced opportunity list, and draft introductions.

Not enabled by default: sending outreach, uploading lists, creating accounts, accepting terms, or spending money.

Every new account should be created one at a time, tested with invented data first, and given a named owner plus a removal path. Account creation itself is an external action and must be explicitly approved before it happens.

## Recommended first activation order

1. **Support first:** connect `hello@mythicalvoid.com` and `parents@mythicalvoid.com` so incoming questions can be sorted and urgent safeguarding concerns can be highlighted. Kevin reviews every reply before it is sent.
2. **Measurement second:** confirm the Google property and keep measurement limited to consent-based, anonymous website totals.
3. **Content third:** add the owned social accounts so the system can prepare drafts from approved Mythical facts. Kevin approves every post and public reply.
4. **Partners last:** only after the first three are working should the system research possible partners or customers. It may prepare a shortlist and draft an introduction, but it must not contact anyone without approval.

This order lets Mythical learn from real questions before it increases its public reach. It also keeps children out of direct automated conversations and keeps every external voice human-approved.
