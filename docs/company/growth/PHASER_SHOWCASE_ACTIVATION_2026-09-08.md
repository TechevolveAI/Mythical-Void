## Why this community fits

The official Phaser Showcase exists for finished and work-in-progress games
built with Phaser. Its current welcome post asks work-in-progress topics to use
`[WIP]` in the title and asks creators to link to their game. Recent Showcase
topics were visible in 2026, so this is a living technical community rather
than an abandoned directory.

That activity does not guarantee views, replies or players. It only makes the
community a sensible place to ask people who understand browser games for
specific, practical feedback.

that seven-day read is complete, or until Kevin explicitly cancels that route.
