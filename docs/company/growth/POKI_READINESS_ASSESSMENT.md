# Is Mythical Void ready for Poki?

**Answer:** promising, but not ready to submit.
**Checked:** 26 August 2026
**External action:** none

## The good news

The private portal review reached a clear opening immediately: **FIRST
CONTACT**, with one egg to tap or click. It filled a 1280 × 720 desktop, a 390 ×
844 phone and an 820 × 1180 tablet-sized screen without page overflow. The
phone showed the correct tap instruction.

The first download is also better than the archive size suggested. The 18
opening resources are about **3.75 MB** using a repeatable gzip estimate. Poki's
current engine guidance describes 5 MB as a good initial-download ceiling.

That means the basic web technology and the new opening are credible. This is
worth preparing seriously.

## Why we should not approach Poki yet

| Question | Current truth | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does the game reach something playable quickly? | Yes: one clear egg interaction | Provisional pass |
| Is the first download lean? | About 3.75 MB estimated | Provisional pass |
| Is the whole delivery lean? | About 41 MB estimated against Poki's 8 MB guidance | Needs major progressive loading |
| Does it work without outside services? | The bundle still contains NASA, Open Notify and Mythical-hosted function routes | Needs a true portal mode |
| Does it fill desktop, phone and tablet screens? | Yes in the private layout review | Provisional pass |
| Are real tablet touch controls proven? | Not yet | Test required |
| Does saving survive blocked storage or private browsing? | Not proven on this package | Test required |
| Is the Poki game-event and advertising layer present? | No—and it is not authorized yet | Later, behind a separate approval |
| Do we have a truthful thumbnail and game images? | No: the human gameplay gate is still 0 of 4 | Blocked |
| Do unfamiliar players enjoy the first minute? | No recorded five-person test yet | Test required |

The main weight is not game code. Cinematics account for about **24.5 MB** and
audio about **9.3 MB** of the estimated compressed delivery. The opening crash
video alone is about **2.73 MB**, making it the largest part of the first load.

## What the game team should do

1. **Make a real portal edition.** The core adventure must work with live NASA,
   Open Notify, hosted AI, cloud saving and Mythical-hosted functions switched
   off. No optional service failure should interrupt play.
2. **Download the world gradually.** Keep the opening and first playable area
   small. Fetch later films, music and realm art only after play has begun.
3. **Prove hostile-browser survival.** Test private browsing, denied storage,
   an ad blocker, a real or touch-emulated tablet and slower hardware.
4. **Keep the strong opening.** Preserve the immediate egg interaction, make
   every early film skippable and teach actions visually and gradually.
5. **Keep Poki separate.** Design a Poki-only connection layer, but install no
   SDK and activate no advertising until Kevin, privacy and commercial review
   approve it.
6. **Earn the click honestly.** Return four excellent real gameplay moments and
   a text-free, full-bleed square image of at least 628 × 628 that closely
   resembles the actual game.

## The later Kevin decision

When those checks pass, ask one question:

> Should we send one bounded Poki access request first for the chance of much
> larger curated reach, or publish the prepared itch.io release first for
> faster independent learning?

The Poki-first route remains my recommendation for maximum upside. Poki says
its preferred deal is web-exclusive, normally for an indicative five years,
and can include marketing to its 100M+ player reach. Exact terms would still
require separate commercial and legal review. Nothing in this assessment
authorizes an account, upload, form submission, SDK, advertising, terms or
exclusivity.
