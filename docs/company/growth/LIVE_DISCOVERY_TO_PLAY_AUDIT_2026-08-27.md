# Live first-impression audit

**Checked:** 27 August 2026

## Outcome

The owned website now does its job well: it looks distinctive, explains the
creature promise quickly and offers an obvious way to start on desktop and
phone. The game also loaded from the public Play link without a sampled browser
error.

The first returning-player view inside the game is not yet good enough for a
large public launch. It is dark, crowded and difficult to understand at a
glance. On phone, a large dark panel and several messages compete with the
world. On desktop, the astronaut and creature overlap into a block-like shape,
while several interface items compete for attention.

This is now a game-presentation problem, not a homepage-redesign problem.

## What was checked

- live homepage at 1280 × 720 and 390 × 844;
- live “find your game” page at 390 × 844;
- the public Play handoff into the game;
- one existing saved-adventure return on desktop and phone;
- visible warning and error messages from the sampled pages.

The existing saved state was not opened or inspected. No saved name, player
detail, screenshot or browser data has been added to the repository.

## What worked

- The desktop homepage becomes a strong creature-universe scene once its main
  picture appears.
- The phone homepage keeps the logo, promise, Play action and creature artwork
  readable in the first screen.
- The game-finder page plainly says free, no download, no account and early
  access before its optional mood choices.
- The Play link reached the live game and the sampled game load completed
  without a visible browser warning or error.
- Returning-player wording correctly changes Play to Continue when a saved
  adventure is present.

## What must improve before promotion

### 1. Make the creature unmistakable

The player and creature must read as two living characters immediately. They
cannot merge into a small square or block-like cluster. The creature needs a
clear silhouette, visible movement and enough separation from the astronaut
and nearby scenery.

### 2. Give the return one main message

The sanctuary check-in, a crystal notice and a living-form notice appeared
around the same return. The first seconds should have one obvious action. Other
messages can wait until the player has moved again.

### 3. Fix the phone composition

The phone return view is dominated by a dark rectangular layer, with parts of
the game interface sitting behind or outside it. The world, creature, action
and button must fit together without looking like a desktop screen squeezed
into a phone.

### 4. Use “creature” consistently

The live return view still includes “companion profile” and “Companion Link.”
Public Mythical language now uses **creature**. The game interface should use
the same word unless Game Development records a deliberate story reason for a
different term.

### 5. Prove both beginnings

This review used an existing saved adventure. It does not prove the clean
first-time route. Before promotion, separately review:

- a completely fresh visitor from Play to the first meaningful action; and
- a returning visitor from Continue to clear control of the creature.

## Release decision

The website remains live. Outside gameplay-led promotion remains closed at
0 of 4 approved moments. Do not use this returning scene as a screenshot,
thumbnail, trailer opening or platform-listing image.

The next game build should return one short desktop and phone proof for the
fresh route and the returning route. A person should be able to say what the
creature is doing and what the player should do next without an explanation.
