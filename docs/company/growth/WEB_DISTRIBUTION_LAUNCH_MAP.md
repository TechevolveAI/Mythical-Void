# Where Mythical Void should appear when someone wants a new game

**Checked:** 27 August 2026
**Current position:** keep the important options open; publish nowhere new yet

## The simple answer

Mythical Void has two realistic first moves once the game looks excellent in
real play:

1. **Ask Poki to consider it first** for a chance at much larger web reach.
2. **Publish on itch.io first** for the fastest route to a real indie game shelf
   and early learning.

The tested itch.io game package is ready. The artwork is not: the authentic
gameplay gate remains at **0 of 4 approved moments**.

A third, unusually valuable route is now worth preparing: **YouTube
Playables**. It could let somebody discover and start Mythical Void inside
YouTube itself. The current Phaser package fits the published hard size limits,
but Playables requires a separate general-audience 13+ edition with no outside
calls, no in-game sharing and YouTube-managed saving. No interest request has
been made. The full assessment is in
[`YOUTUBE_PLAYABLES_READINESS_ASSESSMENT.md`](YOUTUBE_PLAYABLES_READINESS_ASSESSMENT.md).

## What the Poki check found

The opening is promising. A private local review reached the **FIRST CONTACT**
egg interaction immediately on desktop and phone, and the first download is
about **3.75 MB compressed**. That is inside Poki's current suggestion of about
5 MB for the first download.

The complete delivery is not ready. It is about **41 MB compressed**, compared
with Poki's current suggestion of about 8 MB for a good web game. Most of the
extra weight is later films and music. The build also still knows about outside
NASA, Open Notify and Mythical-hosted services; real tablet touch, denied
storage and five unfamiliar players' first minutes have not yet been proved.

The decision is therefore **not ready to submit, preserve the option**. The
exact evidence and game-team request are in
[`POKI_READINESS_ASSESSMENT.md`](POKI_READINESS_ASSESSMENT.md).

## The important choice we nearly missed

Poki says its preferred partnership is web-exclusive. Its current guide says
the same game would normally appear only on Poki across the open web, with an
indicative five-year term. Steam, mobile stores and consoles can remain outside
that restriction. Exact terms would depend on an actual agreement.

Poki also describes a non-exclusive route for games already live elsewhere,
but that route is a one-time flat licence rather than the marketing and revenue
partnership attached to its preferred exclusive deal.

That means publishing on itch.io first is not just a page upload. It can change
the kind of Poki opportunity available later.

## My recommendation

Keep both doors open while the real gameplay is being improved. When the four
visual moments, opening minutes, mobile play and loading all pass review:

- ask Kevin whether to make one bounded Poki access request first;
- submit nothing and accept no terms without that decision;
- if Poki is not interested, is too slow, or offers unsuitable terms, publish
  the already prepared itch.io release;
- if Kevin values immediate learning more than preserving a possible exclusive
  deal, choose itch.io immediately.

For maximum upside, I recommend the Poki access request first. It preserves a
large option while itch.io remains a ready fallback.

## What each route needs

| Route | Why people are there | What Mythical needs | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Poki | A very large audience is actively choosing a web game | Excellent opening play, mobile quality, fast loading, truthful square thumbnail, account details, agreement review | Valuable option preserved; not submitted |
| itch.io | People deliberately browse for unusual independent games | The ready ZIP, truthful cover, real screenshots, accurate tags and AI disclosure, Kevin-controlled account | Technically ready; not published |
| YouTube Playables | People can discover and immediately play games inside YouTube | Honest 13+ audience decision, separate no-network build, YouTube saving and controls, touch/resize proof, official channel | High-upside preparation route; not requested |
| YouTube | People compare gameplay and decide what looks worth trying | Four excellent real moments, a clear official channel and comment coverage | Held for visual quality |
| Newgrounds | People play and judge new HTML5 games | Strong first minutes, correct credits and rights, real presentation and an account | Technically plausible; quality gated |
| Browser-game communities | People deliberately try links recommended by other humans | Genuine participation, current-rule review and a good game—not an automated advert | Relationship gated |
| Game Jolt | Players follow games and creators over time | Current signed-in upload check, identity, a content rhythm and moderation | Further verification needed |
| CrazyGames | People choose browser games immediately | Platform, SDK, advertising, privacy, rights and commercial review | Later candidate |
| Steam | People browse with strong download or buying intent | A downloadable release and a very different store/support plan | Not now |

## Quality rules before any doorway opens

- The creature must be easy to see and must remain convincingly present during
  play.
- A new player must understand the first meaningful action quickly.
- The game must work on desktop and mobile for any large web portal pitch.
- Store images must resemble the real game. The imagined creature-universe art
  cannot be called gameplay.
- Rights, credits and generative-AI disclosures must be ready for the chosen
  platform.
- No one contacts children directly, buys reach, accepts terms or publishes
  through this plan.

## Why itch.io is still first if speed wins

It remains the nearest match to the build we have today. The package opens
directly into the game, passed the nested-frame test, contains 85 files and is
about 47 MB unpacked. itch.io's current guidance also makes the first public
release important: a project receives its one “Most Recent” moment when first
published, and a weak or incomplete page should not be launched early.
