# Three places that actively want to discover unusual games

Status: three tailored drafts are ready; nobody has been contacted and the
public game must be restored first.

## Why this is different from a press list

This is not a spreadsheet of scraped names. Each route has a current public
page inviting game suggestions. Each gets its own short message because the
reason Mythical Void fits is different.

The pitch is not “we used AI.” The pitch is a free browser adventure where a
player hatches an alien creature, crosses six strange realms and makes a final
choice. The father-and-son beginning makes it human. AI and NASA-sourced
learning moments add interest after the game is clear.

## The order

Its official submission page asks for games still in development and says
public access or downloads must be free. That is a close match for Mythical
Void's early-access browser game. It says every submission is read, but it does
not promise coverage.

## How this runs

1. Recheck the official website and prove `/play/` still works at action time.
2. Finish the first r/WebGames seven-day read, or Kevin deliberately cancels
   it.
3. Kevin chooses one of these three exact messages and confirms the adult
   sending address and reply owner.
4. Recheck the destination's current submission page and every Mythical Void
   link.
5. Kevin approves that exact message at the moment of sending.
6. Send one message. Wait seven days before considering a different outlet.

No bulk sending, automatic replies or copied follow-ups. At most one follow-up
may be prepared for separate approval. No screenshots or films are attached
until a person approves the exact real gameplay asset. Generated artwork is
never presented as gameplay.

Record only what actually happens: sending time, a visible delivery failure, a
reply, or a public coverage link. An email does not prove it was opened. A site
visit is not a player. Coverage does not prove a play, and silence does not
prove rejection.
