# Adult-run game discovery shortlist

**Checked:** 27 August 2026
**State:** research only; no pitch, form, email, account or post authorized

## What this list is for

These are places where adults actively introduce players to independent games.
They are more useful than sending the same announcement to hundreds of random
accounts because each has a visible reason to consider Mythical Void.

Nobody should be approached until the game has four excellent real gameplay
moments, a clean playable link, a truthful short description and a named adult
available to answer any public response.

## First group to consider

### 1. Alpha Beta Gamer — strongest early fit

Alpha Beta Gamer explicitly invites games that are still in development. Its
public rules say the game or signup/download must be free, and it asks for
footage and images where possible. Mythical Void's free browser build is a good
structural fit.

**Wait for:** 4/4 visuals, one strong short gameplay video and Kevin approval
of one personal submission. Coverage is never guaranteed.

### 2. IndieGameInn — strong fit if Kevin wants to tell the story himself

IndieGameInn publishes original developer or reviewer presentations at no
publication fee. It accepts careful generative-AI-assisted work but rejects
low-effort AI asset collections. Its current rules require a five-to-ten-minute
presentation, cleared media, human commentary preferred and an active pinned
reply after publication.

This could suit the father-and-son beginning and the actual craft behind the
creature engine, but it needs Kevin's real voice and a time when he can cover
replies. It is not a route for an automated AI voice or a weak trailer.

### 3. Indie Games Developer — useful written discovery route

Its public submission page offers free editorial consideration for independent
games while warning that the queue can be long and coverage is not guaranteed.
This could carry the browser-game, studio and technology story after the proof
pack is strong.

**Wait for:** verified current submission fields, approved screenshots and a
complete claims-and-rights pack.

### 4. I Dream of Indie Games — relevant audience, route still unverified

The outlet remains active and says its purpose is to bring visibility to small
independent games through reviews, lists and news. No sufficiently clear public
submission route was verified in this review, so we should not guess an email
or scrape a personal contact.

**Next step:** check only its current official route when the visual gate
passes; do nothing if it does not invite submissions.

### 5. Best Indie Games / ClemmyGames — strong discovery, weaker current fit

The channel and Steam curator remain active and ran 2026 indie showcases. Its
visible discovery activity is heavily Steam-oriented, while Mythical Void is a
browser game. Keep it as a later possibility rather than forcing a poor pitch.

**Next step:** reconsider when Mythical has a suitable store presence or a
verified browser-game submission route.

## Respectful exclusions

### Wholesome Games — do not pitch the current project

Its values and hopeful-game audience look attractive, but its current official
site states that it stands against AI-generated art. Mythical Void openly uses
generative AI in its creative process. Respect that boundary and do not submit
or try to argue around it.

### Wanderbots — do not contact

The current public channel description says not to contact the creator about a
game made with generative AI. Mythical must respect that clear preference.

## The one-pitch rule

When a route eventually opens, send one tailored, truthful pitch from a named
adult. Include:

- why the game fits that specific audience;
- one direct playable link;
- one sentence explaining the actual game;
- three approved real images and one short approved video;
- honest AI and NASA/STEM wording;
- a clear statement that coverage is optional and no reply is owed.

Do not send reminders automatically, hide the use of AI, buy praise, offer a
child interview, or attach the imagined creature-universe illustration as
gameplay.
