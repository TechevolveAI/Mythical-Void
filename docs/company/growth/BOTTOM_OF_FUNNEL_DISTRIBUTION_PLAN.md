# The shortest route from “I want a new game” to Mythical Void

Status: working plan, checked 26 August 2026

## The decision we need to win

People at this point are not looking for a studio, a technology story or a long explanation. They already want something to play. Mythical Void must answer five questions almost instantly:

1. Is this my kind of game?
2. What is different about it?
3. Does it look like the real game?
4. Can I begin now?
5. Can I trust it?

Our clearest answer is:

> A free browser adventure where you hatch a strange alien creature, cross six living realms and decide what Project Beacon should tell Earth. No download or account needed.

The father-and-son beginning, creature engine, careful use of AI and NASA-inspired STEM material make the world more interesting after that first promise. They should not delay the play decision.

## Why itch.io is first

It is the closest match for the game we have today. itch.io supports games that play directly in the browser. Its official guide says the project needs a ZIP with a top-level `index.html`, no more than 1,000 files, no more than 500 MB extracted, and relative file paths. A free account can create the page.

The public listing still waits for two things:

- four approved, authentic gameplay moments, including one clean creature reveal;
- a browser package tested inside an embedded frame, with no broken absolute paths.

This is a quality gate, not a marketing delay. A bad thumbnail or an inaccurate screenshot loses the player at exactly the point we are trying to win.

## The itch.io listing draft

**Title:** Mythical Void

**Short description:** Hatch a strange alien creature, restore six living realms and decide what Project Beacon should tell Earth.

**Opening line:** Crash beyond mapped space. Discover what survived. Play free in your browser with no download or account.

**Suggested tags to verify in the listing editor:** Adventure, Browser, Creatures, Family Friendly, Science Fiction, Story Rich, Phaser

**Price:** Free, with no donation request in the first release.

**Cover:** A truthful, striking image that resembles the actual creature and game world. itch.io recommends 630 × 500 pixels.

**Screenshots:** Three to five real game images. Do not use imagined universe art as gameplay.

**Controls:** State the real keyboard, mouse and touch controls only after the packaged build is checked.

**Honest notes:** Early access; browser game; no NASA endorsement; generative AI is part of the creative process; important story and safety decisions remain human-led.

## The first-minute test

Before any large portal application, five people unfamiliar with the project should be able to:

- see the creature rather than a placeholder or square;
- understand what to do without a long explanation;
- reach a meaningful action quickly;
- recognise that their creature can be different;
- leave knowing why they might return.

We record what happened, not what we hoped happened. No child is contacted privately and no identifying child information is collected.

## What Kevin will eventually need to approve

No action is needed now. When the visual gate passes, ask Kevin for one decision:

> Shall I publish the prepared itch.io page through an account you control?

That approval covers the public listing and the platform terms. It does not cover ads, paid promotion, direct messages, bulk outreach or additional platforms.
