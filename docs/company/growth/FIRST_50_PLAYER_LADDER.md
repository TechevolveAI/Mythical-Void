# The first 50 people we invite to play

**State:** prepared; invitations wait for the First Five gate and Kevin's approval

## The idea

Mythical Void should not jump from nobody knowing the game to broadcasting it
everywhere. We will learn in three small steps: 5 people, then 15, then 50.
Each step earns the next one.

This is an invitation count, not a claim about players, customers or audience
size. Somebody who receives a link may decide not to play, and anonymous site
events cannot be joined together to identify a person.

## The route people receive

> Hatch a strange alien creature, cross six living realms and decide what
> Project Beacon should tell Earth. Mythical Void is free in your browser—no
> download or account needed.

The link goes to `https://mythicalvoid.com/playable-now/`. From there a person
can play in one click. After playing, an adult can use the fixed-choice page at
`https://mythicalvoid.com/feedback/`. People can also share the clean game link
or try the Hatch Challenge with someone they already know.

## Step 1 — five observed adults

Run the existing First Five playtest with three desktop/laptop sessions and two
phone sessions. Do not promote the game until that gate passes. This catches a
missing creature, confusing first action, unusable phone layout or another
serious presentation problem before it is multiplied.

## Step 2 — fifteen invitations

After the First Five passes, Kevin approves one exact invitation and sends it
to fifteen adults or guardians through relationships he already has. No child
is recruited. There are no automated messages, scraped contacts, mailing lists
or repeated reminders.

Keep the message identical so the result is about the game rather than fifteen
different promises. Watch only anonymous totals: visits to the play page, Play
presses, first hatches, clean-link shares and fixed-choice adult feedback.

Pause if the feedback repeatedly says the creature is hard to see, starting is
confusing, controls are unusable, the page feels unsafe or the public visuals
do not resemble the game. Fix the cause before inviting more people.

## Step 3 — fifty invitations

Only expand the same truthful invitation to a total of fifty after:

- the First Five gate has passed;
- no repeated severe trust, safety or presentation issue appears in the first
  fifteen invitations;
- the real creature reveal and one meaningful action are fit to show publicly;
- Kevin approves the final message and the place it will be shared.

The first public route should be one place where people are already choosing a
game—not simultaneous posts everywhere. The current choice is Poki-first for
maximum platform upside or itch.io-first for faster learning. That decision is
separate and still belongs to Kevin because it can involve platform terms.

## What success means

Success is not likes or follower counts. It means people who were looking for
a game:

1. understood the promise;
2. pressed Play;
3. reached the creature and a meaningful action;
4. wanted to continue, recommend it or share the clean link.

Anonymous events describe separate actions, not a tracked person's journey.
We must not turn those totals into a made-up conversion rate. Adult feedback
is voluntary, uses fixed choices, asks for no name or email address, and is kept
for no more than 180 days.

## Authority boundary

This plan does not authorize invitations, posts, direct messages, paid ads,
platform submissions, platform terms or contact with children. The owned play
and adult-feedback pages may remain live. Kevin approves the exact invitation
and the first external destination when the quality gate passes.
