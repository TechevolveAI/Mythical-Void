# The Hatch Challenge

## The growth idea

Mythical Void already has a reason for one player to tell another player about
it: the creature engine can produce different forms, colours, markings,
personalities, affinities and mutations.

The Hatch Challenge turns that into one simple invitation:

> Same signal. Two creatures. What will hatch?

One person sends a clean link to someone they already know. Both begin in their
own browser, reach the first hatch and compare what appeared.

## What it is not

- It is not multiplayer.
- It does not guarantee that every possible result is unique.
- It does not create an account or a public creature profile.
- It does not upload the creature, the save or anybody’s name.
- Mythical Void does not learn who received the invitation.

The Continue button remains available for an existing player, and Start my
hatch always uses the clean owned `/play/` route.

## Why this comes before social accounts

The challenge gives the game a person-to-person growth loop on a channel
Mythical already owns. It can be tested honestly before the studio opens a
social account, publishes a promotional post or spends money.

Only consented aggregate website events already in the measurement contract
may count Play and Share button use. The game itself is not measured.

## Release gate

The owned website may publish this invitation. No external post, message,
account change or paid promotion is authorized. The separate gameplay visual
gate remains at 0 of 4.
