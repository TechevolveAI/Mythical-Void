**Full description:**

Mythical Void is a free single-player browser adventure. Wanderer-77 has
crashed beyond mapped space. Recover what survived, hatch an alien creature
shaped by a genetics system, then run, leap, explore and battle through six
damaged realms. Rescue the Guardians, build a sanctuary and decide what Project
Beacon should tell Earth.

The game is in early access and starts with no download or account.
Generative AI supports parts of our small Irish studio's creative process,
while people remain responsible for the story, safety, testing and what is
released. Mythical Void began as a father-and-son project built from
imagination, questions and experiments at home.

Optional learning moments use credited public NASA material. Mythical Void is
independent and is not made, approved or endorsed by NASA.

**What players do:**

- Hatch and name an alien creature shaped by a genetics system.
- Run, leap, explore and battle through six different realms.
- Rescue corrupted Guardians and bring life back to their worlds.
- Build a sanctuary and make a final choice about Project Beacon.
- Play free in a modern browser with no download or account needed to start.

**AI disclosure:** Generative AI supports parts of the studio's creative
process. People remain responsible for the story, safety, testing and what is
released.

**NASA disclosure:** Optional learning moments use credited public NASA
material. Mythical Void is independent and is not made, approved or endorsed
by NASA.

**How it began:** Mythical Void began in Ireland as a father-and-son project: a
parent and his young son exploring what imagination and generative AI tools
could turn into a real game.
