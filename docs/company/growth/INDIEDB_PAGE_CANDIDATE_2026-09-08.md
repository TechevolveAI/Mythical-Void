## Ready-to-paste page draft

**Title:** Mythical Void

**Status:** Early access / in development

**Platform:** Web browser

**Engine:** Phaser 3

**Genres to confirm in the signed-in form:** Adventure; Platformer

**Official website:** https://mythicalvoid.com/

**Play now:** https://mythicalvoid.com/play/

**Short description:** Hatch a genetically varied alien creature, cross six
living realms and decide what Project Beacon should tell Earth.

**Opening:** Crash beyond mapped space. Discover what survived. Mythical Void
is a free browser adventure with no download or account needed to start.

**Full description:**

Wanderer-77 has crashed beyond mapped space. Recover the field kit, read a
strange message and hatch an alien creature shaped by genetics, cosmic affinity
and rare changes.

Cross six living realms, free their guardians from the Void, build a sanctuary
and decide what Project Beacon should tell Earth.

Mythical Void is a free, single-player browser adventure in early access.
Generative AI is part of our small studio's creative process, but people remain
responsible for the story, safety, public claims and important choices. Some
optional learning moments use clearly credited public NASA material. Mythical
Void is independent and is not made, approved or endorsed by NASA.

**What players do:**

- Hatch and care for a creature shaped by procedural genetics.
- Run, leap, explore and fight through six living realms.
- Free guardians from the corruption holding them.
- Build a sanctuary and decide what Project Beacon should become.
- Play free in a modern browser with no download or account needed to start.

**How the studio began:**

Mythical Void began in Ireland as a father-and-son project: a parent and his
young son exploring what imagination and generative AI tools could make
possible. Their conversations grew into strange creatures, living worlds and a
playable adventure.

**AI disclosure:** Generative AI supports parts of the studio's creative
process and some optional expressive features. People remain responsible for
the story, safety, public claims and important choices.

**NASA disclosure:** Some optional learning moments use clearly credited public
NASA material. Mythical Void is independent and is not made, approved or
endorsed by NASA.
