## Purpose

A-032 turns public commercial research into consistent internal pursue, defer,
or decline reasoning without fabricating demand, reach, revenue, probability,
pricing, or partner interest. The source is
[`commercial/qualification.json`](commercial/qualification.json).

It is designed for distribution, publishing, creator, press, licensing, brand,
education/culture, grant, sponsorship, and technology opportunities. The first
portfolio covers the four researched browser-distribution candidates already
registered in A-007.

## Qualification model

Every assessment keeps ten judgments visible: audience, product, trust/safety,
strategic learning, reach quality, economics, control/rights, integration,
operational load, and reversibility. Each judgment names whether it comes from
a public source, internal inference, or missing evidence.

There is no composite score. A rank means only the sequence for the next
reversible internal evidence-gathering step. It is not a forecast, endorsement,
or estimate of market attractiveness.

An opportunity cannot become `qualified` until it has current official-source
review, no unresolved automatic disqualifier, named ownership, audience and
product evidence, technical feasibility, privacy/safeguarding and rights/terms
review, support capacity, a financial decision boundary, and Kevin's decision.

## Automatic disqualifiers

The deal desk stops progression for:

1. behavioral advertising or profiling of children;
2. sale, enrichment, or unrelated reuse of player data;
3. open child messaging or ungoverned user-generated content;
4. undisclosed sponsorship or deceptive acquisition;
5. unapproved training on Mythical IP, gameplay, or customer data;
6. broad IP transfer, perpetual exclusivity, or unclear derivatives;
7. unreviewed SDK changes to privacy, security, performance, or experience;
8. pressure to misstate release, audience, AI, safety, or traction.

“Not observed in public research” is not clearance. Clearance requires linked
contract, policy, technical, privacy, security, rights, and human review
evidence appropriate to the issue.

## Deal-desk handoff

Only after qualification may an outreach package be drafted. It must bind the
organization and purpose to a public role-based recipient source, approved
claims and authentic proof, sender/channel/message/window, maximum one useful
follow-up, measurement, stop conditions, and trusted A-011 approval evidence.

Pricing, terms, build sharing, SDKs, data, advertising, IP rights, exclusivity,
spend, proposal delivery, acceptance, signature, and launch remain separate
human and professional-review decisions. Conversation is not authorization.
