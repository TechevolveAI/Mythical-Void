# Sanctuary Grown-Tech Asset Provenance

Status: private implementation candidate; not approved for production

## Scope

This record covers the first grown-tech world-art pair only:

- `public/game/village/world/grown-tech/village-heart-grown-v2.webp`
- `public/game/village/world/grown-tech/compact/village-heart-grown-v2.png`
- `public/game/village/world/grown-tech/forager-hut-grown-v2.webp`
- `public/game/village/world/grown-tech/compact/forager-hut-grown-v2.png`

## Creation

The assets were generated privately on 2026-09-14 with the Codex built-in
image generation tool. The approved internal concept
`sanctuary-heart-forager-concept-v1.png` was supplied only as a material,
lighting and camera reference. The prompts required isolated transparent
sprites and explicitly excluded conventional flowers, cottages, portals,
shrines, circular platforms and terrain rectangles.

The Village Heart prompt specified a low alien supply organ with three unequal
chambers, a central pulse organ, non-Earth root channels and one small
red-black-white-green Wanderer-77 field brace.

The Forager Hut prompt specified a seed-regrowth shelter with three growth
stages, an open helper hollow, a visible seed-return Current and one small
Wanderer-77 scanner. It excluded doors, roofs, windows, baskets, ordinary
fruit, leaves and timber.

## Processing

The generated PNGs retained their alpha channels. Project assets were resized
without changing aspect ratio. World variants were encoded as WebP at quality
86 with alpha quality 95; compact variants remain RGBA PNGs. Runtime display
sizes preserve each source aspect ratio.

## Rights And Review Boundary

The assets were created for Mythical Void within the project's authorised
image-generation workflow and contain no third-party logos, characters or
personal data. They have not received final human visual approval. They must
remain private until phone and desktop gameplay evidence confirms that they
read as living planetary structures, fit the Sanctuary world, and remain clear
beside the actual player and creature.
